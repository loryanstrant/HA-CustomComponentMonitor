# Dismiss in HACS too — v1.17.2

## Fixed
- Dismissing an item in **New in HACS** also clears its New status in HACS itself.
- Items automatically hidden for another country are cleared there too; the next scan catches up items already rejected before upgrading.
- **Restore** brings eligible items back in both lists, and hidden-item history stays available after syncing and restarting.
- Review actions refresh the monitor immediately, so a quick Dismiss → Restore does not act on an old list.

## Upgrade notes
Only review status changes: nothing is installed or removed. Unreviewed items stay New.
If HACS is still starting or cannot save, the decision is kept and syncing retries on the next scan.
