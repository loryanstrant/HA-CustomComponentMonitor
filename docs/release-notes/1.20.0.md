## What's new in v1.20.0

A scan that fails no longer blanks your cards. The counts you already had stay on screen, marked **Stale**, with the reason and when they were last good.

### Fixed
- **All four cards went blank when a scan failed, even though the data was fine.** A failed scan took all six sensors unavailable, so every card switched to its "no data" branch. One real case made that permanent: HACS 2.0.5 can get stuck reporting itself as *starting* for the whole time Home Assistant is up — its repository file on disk is complete and correct, but the monitor refused to read it, every scan failed, and all four cards showed nothing for days. The monitor now reads that file and judges the data rather than the status flag.
- **A permanently failing scan no longer retries every 30 seconds forever.** The retry backs off — 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, and so on up to the ordinary hourly scan — and resets to normal the moment a scan succeeds. The matching error is logged once per distinct message instead of twice a minute for the life of the uptime.

### Added
- **A "Stale" notice on every card.** When the newest scan failed, each card keeps showing the last good scan and says so: *◐ **Stale** — showing the last good scan from 2 hours ago. The newest scan failed: HACS is starting; component data will retry automatically.* The three cards with a header badge row also carry a small **Stale** badge beside the count. The mark is a drawn half-filled shape paired with the word, so nothing depends on telling colours apart.
- **Four new attributes on all six sensors** — `data_stale`, `last_successful_scan`, `scan_error` and `store_status` — so an automation can notify you that the monitor has been stuck, and so "why is it stuck" can be answered from the entity rather than the log. `store_status` reports the active store's own view of itself (`provider`, `loaded`, `disabled`, `stage`, `starting`).

### Upgrade notes
- Restart Home Assistant after updating, then **hard-refresh your dashboard** to load the updated cards (all four are bumped to 1.20.0).
- **Stale is not forever.** Last-good data is served for up to 24 hours. Past that the sensors do go unavailable and the cards show **Unavailable** again, because "these numbers are from last week" is worse than an honest blank.
- **A cold start still shows Unavailable.** If the first scan after a restart fails there is no earlier data to be stale about, so the old behaviour is unchanged there. The two states are never shown together.
- If you are seeing the Stale notice with *"… is starting"* in it, the fault is in your store integration's startup, not here. `store_status` on any of the sensors will show `starting: true` with a `stage` of `startup`; a Home Assistant restart clears it, and Home Assistant 2026.11's built-in Marketplace replaces the HACS version this happens on.
- No dashboard changes are needed. Sensor IDs, card types, service names and to-do history are unchanged.
