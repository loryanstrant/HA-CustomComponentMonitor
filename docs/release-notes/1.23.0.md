## What's new in v1.23.0
Three fixes, all of them things that made the cards annoying or quietly wrong. Two were reported; the third was found while chasing the second.

### Fixed
- **Updating a component with an unknown requirement is one click again.** v1.19.0 added a "I understand the requirement is unknown — install anyway" tick-box that you had to click before the Update button would work. Most components declare no minimum Home Assistant version at all, so that was an extra click on most rows, for a warning the component never acted on anyway. The tick-box is gone. The orange **Requirement unknown** panel is unchanged — you still get told what could not be established, you just don't have to acknowledge it first.
- **"Update all" now includes those components.** It used to leave every unknown-requirement row out of the bulk set and read `Update 5 of 8`. It now reads `Update all (8)` and installs them, with the line beside it changed from `3 skipped — requirement unknown` to `3 with an unknown requirement`. Rows that genuinely need a newer Home Assistant are still held back, still counted as `N blocked`, and still have no Update button at all.
- **Dismissing in New in HACS works again.** Since v1.22.0 neither the X on a row nor **Dismiss all** made anything disappear. The dismissal was being saved correctly — it just never reached the screen, so the row stayed put until the next hourly scan. v1.22.0 changed dismiss to edit the published list in place, and Home Assistant only sends an attribute to your browser when its value has *changed*; a list edited in place still compares equal to itself, so the update was dropped on the way out. The sensor now publishes a fresh copy each time.
- **New in HACS shows real ages again.** Every row said it was 0 days old, which made the newest-first ordering meaningless and meant nothing could ever age out of the 30-day window. The housekeeping rule that bounds the saved history was counting dismissed and auto-hidden records towards a limit they are exempt from — so on any store with more than 2,000 of those (easy to reach after a few months of dismissing), it tried to free space on every scan and the only records it was allowed to delete were the currently-visible ones. It deleted them all, every scan, and the next scan stamped them as first seen just now. The limit is now measured only against the records it is actually allowed to delete.

### Upgrade notes
- Restart Home Assistant after updating, then **hard-refresh your dashboard** to load the updated cards (all four are bumped to 1.23.0).
- Ages in New in HACS rebuild themselves from now on, not retroactively. Rows already in the list restart from today; rows that appear after the update age correctly from the day they arrive. Nothing you have dismissed comes back.
- The saved New in HACS history can now be larger than 2,000 records. That is deliberate — dismissals are kept forever on purpose. Records for repositories that vanish from HACS are still removed after 45 days, so it stays bounded.
- No dashboard changes are needed. Sensor IDs, card types, service names and dismissal history are unchanged.
