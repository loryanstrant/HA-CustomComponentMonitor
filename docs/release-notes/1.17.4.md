## What's new in v1.17.4
Themes and templates now appear in your existing New in HACS review card.

### Added
- **Themes and Templates filters** alongside All, Integrations and Cards, including the card's “Open filtered to” setting.
- Themes appear under **Dashboard & theming**. Templates use the existing groups based on what they do.

### Fixed
- Dismissals and automatic country hides clear **New** status in HACS for themes and templates too. **Restore** brings eligible items back to both lists.

### Upgrade notes
- Restart Home Assistant after updating, then refresh your dashboard to load the updated card. Newly supported items appear on the next scan; existing dismissals remain saved.
- Installed-item usage monitoring is unchanged.
